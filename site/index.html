THE CITY IS THE BATTLEFIELD

Sector 7 — a ruined urban grid with two clear boulevards, collapsed housing blocks, a contested central plaza and enough cover to make every street a decision.

Pick a side. Take the centre.

Choose Ukraine or Russia, then an operator, then your controls. You spawn at the rally point with twenty-nine bots on your side and thirty on theirs. Every death costs your team a ticket. When the clock runs out, the side holding more tickets owns the city.

  • Two teams of 30 — you plus 59 AI soldiers
  • Central capture sector with a live frontline meter
  • Kill feed, squad radio and command subtitles
  • Client-side match sim — no install, no launcher

BRIEFING

Is HELL ON EARTH free?

Yes. The beta is free to play in a modern desktop or mobile browser.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The game is built with three.js and loads like a website. Controls and graphics options are in the menu.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Choose Mobile / touch on the operator screen. Landscape orientation is strongly recommended.

Is this a real conflict?

No. HELL ON EARTH is a fictional video game. The soldiers, units and events are fictional and do not represent real persons or events. Voice lines are synthesized.

Why do soldiers shout when they run out of ammo?

Because it is in the design. When a bot or the player empties every magazine, a synthesized voice line calls for a weapon and the unit is resupplied a few seconds later.
